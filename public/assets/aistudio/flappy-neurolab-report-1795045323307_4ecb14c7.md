## 6. Implications for World Models & Complex Agents (e.g. 3D/Minecraft)
- Optimizer Selection under Non-Stationarity: Genetic Algorithm (GA) led overall (100% perfection, avg 50,000 pipes), while Genetic Algorithm (GA) scored 0% on dynamic/gust scenarios — mirroring why World Model planners (e.g. PlaNet/Dreamer latent rollouts) pair well with CEM or momentum-regularized ES when environmental dynamics shift.
- Latent Controller Bottleneck Sizing: Depth 0–1 controllers converged in 8.48s vs 22.59s for Depth 2+ networks. For high-dimensional games like Minecraft, keeping the policy controller narrow (reading compact latent World Model state vectors rather than raw pixels directly) minimizes evolutionary sample complexity.
- Generalization vs. Memorization Ratio: Across 324 trials, unseen-seed worst-case retention averaged 100% of peak training score. Evaluating agents over randomized multi-seed rollouts prevents overfitting to deterministic transition seeds before deploying to open-world tasks.
